# Megacorp PMC dynasties (lore)

**Stashed 2026-07-28.** Design/lore only — not wired into runtime yet.

---

All the megacorps have a bit of inherent tension with the fact that they need to actually run a business to pay for shit but they need an effective fighting force to keep all their shit getting blown up. The problem is that it's really easy for the people with all the guns to start thinking they should call all the shots and if they start doing that corporations tend to collapse due to mismanagement. So as a result the megacorps have arrived at a solution of quasi-outsourcing.

The megacorps have a group of wealthy families that they trust, relationships built up over generations ideally who serve as PMCs for the corporation. These PMC dynasties generally have family ties to people inside the corporate management hierarchy but it's generally expected that you can be a fighter or a manager but if you try to be both at the same time you are de facto stating your desire to conduct a coup. The manager class comes from a mix of old money people trained from childhood and people who were good enough at corporate politics to climb their way up from the ranks. The fighter class of people is almost exclusively old money.

The old money aspect of the PMCs let them act as a leash for their soldiers. The soldiers generally have very little money but their patrons cover all their expenses and put them up in nice digs. If they try to overthrow their boss and steal the payroll there isn't much to steal (and they become squatters in their own home). And no other PMC is going to want to hire traitors. So it's seen as an extremely stupid move to turn on your boss. (I bet you can guess the setup for the next session).

The PMCs are officially all contractors who just happen to only ever take contracts with one corp. Their funding is generally handled through giving them an extremely cushy gig to fund their day to day, something like running a customs post or protecting some facility. This gig goes to the blue bloods not the mercs to create another level of dependence. Of course if they dont actually complete their cushy gig and are in breach of the contract they are going to lose it and their revenue stream. They should have funds to survive the drought (their primary qualification is having money) but losing their gig would be a huge blow to their political standing within the corporation.

At the very top (like Stacy's parents), the executives would be managers who directly hire their own private armies (like genetically engineered super soldiers) but below that level having too many bodyguards is seen as a sign that you are up to something.
