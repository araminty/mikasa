Implementation roadmap:
Assets could be tagged as linked to these industries (can also not be tagged anything in particular)
Self-sufficiency is tracked by the number of import substitution tags that have at least one asset
self-sufficiency gives a bonus to industries tracked internal, a smaller boost to ones tagged self-sufficiency and a penalty to ones tagged export
The corporations get annoyed as import substitution gears up
The syndicates get more powerful as import substitution gears up, especially import substitution assets in the Pitts or their home district (so double the pitts for the 7 families)

Once you are far enough along that you are gearing up for hostilities with the corporations, having certain groups support you strongly enough will trigger events where they will offer war industries.  Some of these will be linked to certain industries (so weak if that industry is struggling, stronger if that industry is afluent+has multiple tagged assets).  Others of these wont have an industry requirement but will require strong support from the associated group and may require that group be economically secure enough.  (Clans and drones just require relations)
